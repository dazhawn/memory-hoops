🏀 Memory Hoops

Privacy Policy — Last updated: June 2026

Memory Hoops ("the App") is developed and published by Millionaire Blueprint. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum data necessary to operate the App:

  • Player name (optional) — entered voluntarily when submitting a score to the global leaderboard. Names are publicly visible on the leaderboard.
  • Scores and round numbers — submitted together with your name when you choose to post to the leaderboard.
  • Local high score — stored only on your device using localStorage; never transmitted to our servers.

We do not collect: email addresses, phone numbers, location data, device identifiers, advertising IDs, or any other personal information.

2. Multiplayer (Remote Mode)

When using Remote multiplayer, a temporary game room is created on our server. No personal data is stored during this process. Room sessions are destroyed immediately when both players disconnect.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Display your name and score on the public leaderboard
  • Show your global rank on the leaderboard

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Data Retention

Leaderboard entries are retained indefinitely so the leaderboard remains meaningful. You may request removal of your entry by contacting us (see Section 7).

5. Children's Privacy

Memory Hoops is rated for ages 4 and up. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The only data collected (player name for leaderboard) is entirely optional and user-initiated.

6. Security

Leaderboard data is stored in a secure PostgreSQL database. We use HTTPS for all data transmission between the App and our servers.

7. Your Rights & Contact

You have the right to request deletion of your leaderboard entry at any time. To do so, or for any privacy questions, contact us at:

privacy@millionaireblueprint.com

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance.
